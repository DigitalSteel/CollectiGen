# CollectiGen Train 0.2.0-alpha.1

November 17, 2026 · Alpha

- Package review starts with the goal, model, training method, image scope and
  next action. Full settings, captions and optional checks stay accessible in
  Package details and checks.
- Preparation and saved-trainer checks show activity and elapsed time, with
  safe cancellation. Trainer folders remain available for manual selection.
- Completion identifies the retained workspace and distinguishes a successful
  saved-list entry from a history-save problem. Retrying history does not
  prepare the dataset again.
- Saved Preparations foregrounds readable names and status. Preparation details
  keeps exact identifiers and destinations accessible without crowding the list.
- Model family and training method stay distinct. Existing SDXL, Anima and
  manual-package branches retain their individual support limits; this update
  adds no new model or training method.

Use Manager 0.8.0-alpha.1 with launcher 0.1.1 from the complete Suite distribution.
Update Train through Applications and close it normally before updating or
recovering its installation. Saved work stays separate from application files.
Preparation does not start training. Real training, model quality and hardware
suitability remain separate qualifications.
