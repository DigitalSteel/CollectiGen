# CollectiGen Train 0.1.0-alpha.1

November 17, 2026 · Alpha

- Train has its own application window, installed and opened from Suite
  Manager's Applications section.
- Open a training package and review its resources before preparing an SDXL
  dataset. Preparation shows elapsed time and supports a safe cancellation.
- Saved Preparations reopens prepared datasets and their receipts. Stable
  preparation names stay distinct from training runs, available under More.
- Manager handles installation, updates, shared settings and connections.
  Updates and restarts remain explicit choices.

Use Manager 0.8.0-alpha.1 with launcher 0.1.1 from the complete Suite distribution.
See README.md for startup and recovery guidance. Preparation and history were
checked with generated images and disposable settings. Opening a package or
preparing a dataset does not start training. Real training, model quality and
hardware suitability remain separate checks.
